Delete your Bonsai account

Bonsai · app ID com.harmoniousminds.bonsai

Bonsai only creates an account if you choose to use Partner Mode (a shared tree with a friend). Single-player use needs no account at all. You can delete your account and its data yourself, at any time, from inside the app — no need to contact us.

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Bonsai.
  2. Tap Partner in the row of links at the bottom of the tree screen.
  3. Sign in if you are signed out (or tap Continue without an account).
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm with Yes, delete my account.

Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email harmoniousminds1@gmail.com (or copy the address: harmoniousminds1@gmail.com) with the subject Account deletion request. Tell us the display name you used and, if you know it, your shared tree's invite code. We will delete the account and confirm within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Your account and its identifierDeleted immediately
Your email address (if you signed in with Apple or Google)Deleted immediately
Your display nameDeleted immediately
Your membership of any shared treeDeleted immediately
A shared tree with nobody left in itDeleted immediately
A shared tree your partner still tendsKept, so their tree survives — your membership and name are removed from it
Your single-player tree and habit logsNever left your device; removed when you uninstall the app
Purchase recordsHeld by Google Play / Apple, not by us. Manage these in your store account.

We keep no backups of deleted accounts, and no retention period applies.
